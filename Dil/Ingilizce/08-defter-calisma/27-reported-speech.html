Reported speech — zaman kaydırma, say/tell, soru ve emir aktarma

Birinin sözünü aktarırken (He said…) fiil genellikle bir zaman geri kayar, zamirler ve "bugün/burada" gibi sözcükler konuşma anına göre değişir. Soru aktarımında sıra düzelir.

§ 2Ne zaman, nasıl kullanılır

  • Aktarma fiili present ise kaydırma yok. He says he is tired. — Yorgun olduğunu söylüyor.
  • Hâlâ doğru olan bilgi kaydırılmayabilir. She said the office is closed on Sundays. — Ofisin pazar günleri kapalı olduğunu söyledi.
  • Zaman / yer sözcükleri değişir: now → then, today → that day, yesterday → the day before / the previous day, tomorrow → the next day / the following day, last week → the week before, next week → the following week, ago → before, here → there, this → that.
  • Zamirler konuşana göre değişir. "I like your code." → He said he liked my code.
  • say vs tell. He said (that)… / He said to me… / He told me (that)… — tell nesne ister.
  • Soru aktarımı — do/does/did düşer, sıra düzelir. "Where do you work?" → She asked me where I worked.
  • Yes/No soru → if / whether. "Are you coming?" → He asked if I was coming.
  • Emir → tell + kişi + to V1. "Close the door." → She told me to close the door.
  • Rica → ask + kişi + to V1. "Can you help me?" → He asked me to help him.
  • Diğer aktarma fiilleri: suggest (+ -ing / that), advise sb to, remind sb to, warn sb (not) to, promise to, offer to, refuse to, admit + -ing, deny + -ing, apologise for + -ing, explain that, complain that.

§ 3İpucu kelimeler

  • that konuşmada düşebilir: She said (that) she was busy.
  • Soru aktarımında soru işareti yok: He asked where I lived.
  • ask + kişi + to (rica) · ask + if/wh (soru).

§ 6Alıştırma

A. Dolaylı anlatıma çevir

  1. "I am working from home today." → She said ___
  2. "We have fixed the bug." → They said ___
  3. "I will send the invoice tomorrow." → He said ___
  4. "Do you use Docker?" → She asked me ___
  5. "Where is the meeting room?" → He asked ___
  6. "Don't push to main." → The lead told us ___

B. Çevir

  1. Bana geç kalacağını söyledi.
  2. Neden bu kadar sessiz olduğumu sordu.
  3. Yöneticimiz bize raporu bitirmemizi söyledi.

C. Hatayı bul

  1. She said me she was tired.
  2. He asked me what was my name.
  3. They told that the server was down.
Cevapları göster
  1. She said (that) she was working from home that day. · 2. They said (that) they had fixed the bug. · 3. He said (that) he would send the invoice the next day. · 4. She asked me if/whether I used Docker. · 5. He asked where the meeting room was. · 6. The lead told us not to push to main.
  2. He/She told me (that) he/she would be late.
  3. He/She asked (me) why I was so quiet.
  4. Our manager told us to finish the report.
  5. She told me she was tired. / She said she was tired.
  6. He asked me what my name was.
  7. They said that the server was down. / They told us that…

Konu 16 · Soru, olumsuz ve modallar

Perfect modals — should have, must have, could have, might have

Modal + have + V3, geçmiş hakkında yorum yapar: pişmanlık (should have), kesin çıkarım (must have), olasılık (might have) ve olmayan imkân (could have).

İngilizce Defter Rehberi · B1–B2 · 29 Ekim 2026

§ 2Ne zaman, nasıl kullanılır

  • Pişmanlık / eleştiri. I should have backed up the database. — Veritabanını yedeklemeliydim.
  • Kesin çıkarım. The lights are off. They must have left. — Işıklar kapalı, çıkmış olmalılar.
  • İmkânsızlık çıkarımı. She can't have seen the message; she was offline. — Mesajı görmüş olamaz.
  • Olasılık. He might have forgotten the meeting. — Toplantıyı unutmuş olabilir.
  • Kaçırılan imkân. You could have asked me! — Bana sorabilirdin!
  • Kıl payı kurtuluş. That could have been a disaster. — Felaket olabilirdi.
  • 3. tip koşulun sonucu (Konu 25). If I'd known, I would have helped. — Bilseydim yardım ederdim.
  • Gereksiz yapılan iş. You needn't have bought it; we already had one. — Almana gerek yoktu.

§ 3İpucu kelimeler

  • Pişmanlık bağlamı: I wish…, Why didn't you…?, It was a mistake.
  • Kanıt varsa → must have / can't have; kanıt zayıf → may / might / could have.
  • Konuşmada kısaltma: should've, could've, must've (should of yazılmaz).

§ 6Alıştırma

A. Perfect modal yaz

  1. The road is wet. It ___ (rain) last night. (kesin)
  2. I failed the test. I ___ (study) more.
  3. He ___ (take) your laptop; he was abroad. (imkânsız)
  4. I'm not sure where she is. She ___ (go) home early.
  5. Why did you walk? I ___ (give) you a lift.

B. Çevir

  1. Bunu bana daha önce söylemeliydin.
  2. Toplantıyı unutmuş olabilir.
  3. Cuma günü canlıya almamalıydık.

C. Hatayı bul

  1. You could have tell me.
  2. They must had finished.
  3. She can't has received it yet.
Cevapları göster
  1. must have rained · 2. should have studied · 3. can't have taken · 4. might / may have gone · 5. could have given
  2. You should have told me this earlier.
  3. He/She might have forgotten the meeting.
  4. We shouldn't have deployed to production on Friday.
  5. You could have told me.
  6. They must have finished.
  7. She can't have received it yet.

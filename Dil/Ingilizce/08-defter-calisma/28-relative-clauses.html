Relative clauses — who, which, that, whose, where

Türkçede "dün yazdığım kod" — niteleyen öbek isimden önce gelir. İngilizcede tersine: the code (that) I wrote yesterday — isim önce, açıklama arkadan ilgi zamiriyle gelir.

İngilizce Defter Rehberi · B1–B2 · 29 Ekim 2026

§ 2Ne zaman, nasıl kullanılır

  • Defining (tanımlayan) — hangisi olduğunu belirtir, virgül yok. The laptop that I bought is great. — Aldığım dizüstü harika (başka laptoplar da var).
  • Non-defining (ek bilgi) — virgül var, that yasak. My laptop, which I bought last year, is great. — Geçen yıl aldığım dizüstüm harika (tek laptopum var).
  • Nesne konumundaki zamir defining'de düşebilir. The code (that) I wrote ✓ · The code that crashed — özne olduğu için düşmez.
  • whose — sahiplik, kişi ve şey için. a company whose product I use — ürününü kullandığım bir şirket.
  • where = in/at which. the city where I live = the city in which I live.
  • Edat sona ya da başa. the team (that) I work with (günlük) / the team with whom I work (resmî). Edattan sonra that ve who gelmez.
  • which — bütün cümleye atıf (virgüllü). He fixed it in 5 minutes, which was impressive. — 5 dakikada düzeltti, ki bu etkileyiciydi.
  • Kısaltılmış (participle) cümlecik. the people working here (= who work) · the files uploaded yesterday (= that were uploaded).

§ 3that'in kullanılamadığı yerler

  • Virgüllü (non-defining) cümlede: Tom, that lives in London,… → who.
  • Edattan hemen sonra: the folder in that… → in which.
  • Bütün cümleye atıfta: …, that was great → which.
  • that tercih edilen yerler: all, everything, nothing, anything, only, the first, superlative sonrası: It's the best tool that I've used. Everything that you need is here.

§ 4İpucu kelimeler

  • Virgül var mı? → varsa who / which, that yok, zamir düşmez.
  • Arkasından özne + fiil geliyorsa (nesne konumu) → zamir düşebilir.
  • what = the thing(s) which: What I need is coffee. the thing what.

§ 7Alıştırma

A. who / which / that / whose / where / Ø

  1. This is the colleague ___ helped me with the deploy.
  2. The bug ___ you reported has been fixed.
  3. Berlin, ___ Tom lived for two years, is expensive.
  4. That's the developer ___ code is always clean.
  5. The cloud provider, ___ we've used since 2020, raised prices.
  6. Is this the bag ___ you lost? (zamir düşebilir mi?)

B. Birleştir

  1. I met a woman. She works at Google.
  2. The server is old. We use it for backups.

C. Çevir

  1. Dün yüklediğin dosya bozuk.

D. Hatayı bul

  1. My laptop, that I bought in 2022, is slow.
  2. The guy who's bike was stolen called the police.
  3. This is the restaurant where we had dinner there.
Cevapları göster
  1. who / that · 2. that / which / Ø · 3. where · 4. whose · 5. which · 6. that / which / Ø (evet, nesne konumu)
  2. I met a woman who works at Google.
  3. The server (that/which) we use for backups is old.
  4. The file (that) you uploaded yesterday is corrupted.
  5. My laptop, which I bought in 2022, is slow.
  6. The guy whose bike was stolen called the police.
  7. This is the restaurant where we had dinner. (there atılır)

Past Perfect Simple ve Past Perfect Continuous

Past Perfect, geçmişteki bir andan daha önce olanı anlatır: "ben vardığımda toplantı bitmişti". Geçmişte iki olay varsa ve sıra önemliyse önce olan past perfect olur.

İngilizce Defter Rehberi · B1–B2 · 29 Ekim 2026

§ 1Past Perfect Simple — Deftere yaz

Deftere yaz — Past Perfect Simple

Formül: S + had + V3 (bütün öznelerde had)

KalıpÖrnek
OlumluS + had + V3The meeting had started when I arrived.
OlumsuzS + hadn't + V3The meeting hadn't started when I arrived.
SoruHad + S + V3?Had the meeting started when you arrived?

Anlam: -mıştı: "toplantı başlamıştı".

Ne zaman kullanılır

  • Geçmişteki bir olaydan önce olan. When I logged in, someone had already changed the config. — Giriş yaptığımda biri ayarı çoktan değiştirmişti.
  • by the time / before / after ile sıra. By the time we noticed, the disk had filled up. — Fark ettiğimizde disk dolmuştu.
  • Geçmişte o ana kadarki tecrübe. I had never seen such a bug before. — Daha önce öyle bir hata görmemiştim.
  • Reported speech ve 3. tip koşulda (Konu 25, 27). She said she had sent it. — Gönderdiğini söyledi.

§ 2Past Perfect Continuous — Deftere yaz

Deftere yaz — Past Perfect Continuous

Formül: S + had been + V-ing

KalıpÖrnek
OlumluS + had been + V-ingShe had been working there for 5 years when she quit.
OlumsuzS + hadn't been + V-ingShe hadn't been working there long.
SoruHad + S + been + V-ing?How long had she been working there?

Anlam: -mekteydi / -iyordu (o ana kadar): "istifa ettiğinde 5 yıldır orada çalışıyordu".

Ne zaman kullanılır

  • Geçmişteki bir ana kadar süren süreç (süre vurgusu). We had been waiting for an hour when the bus came. — Otobüs geldiğinde bir saattir bekliyorduk.
  • Geçmişteki bir durumun sebebi olan süreç. His eyes were red because he had been staring at the screen all day. — Bütün gün ekrana baktığı için gözleri kızarmıştı.

§ 3İpucu kelimeler

  • by the time, before, after, when, already, just, never … before, until, by + geçmiş zaman (by 2020)
  • for / since geçmiş bağlamda → past perfect (continuous).
  • after / before sırayı zaten belli ediyorsa past simple da doğrudur: After I finished, I left. = After I had finished, I left.

§ 4Türklerin sık yaptığı hatalar

YanlışDoğruNeden
When I arrived, the train already left.…the train had already left.Önce olan → had + V3.
I had went.I had gone.had + V3.
I had seen him yesterday. (tek olay)I saw him yesterday.Tek olayda past perfect gereksiz.
She told she had finished.She said / told me she had finished.tell nesne ister (Konu 27).
They had been knowing it.They had known it.Stative.

§ 5Karıştırılan çiftler

Past SimplePast Perfect
Sıraardışık: önce gel, sonra çıkbiri diğerinden önce bitmiş
ÖrnekWhen I arrived, he left.When I arrived, he had left.
TürkçeBen gelince o çıktı.Ben geldiğimde o çıkmıştı.
Present PerfectPast Perfect
Referans anşimdigeçmişte bir an
ÖrnekI've finished (şu an hazır).I had finished by 5 pm.

§ 6Alıştırma

A. Past simple / past perfect / past perfect continuous

  1. By the time we arrived, the demo ___ (end).
  2. She ___ (work) on the feature for weeks before it was cancelled.
  3. I ___ (never / use) Linux before I joined this company.
  4. When I opened the PR, someone ___ (already / approve) it.
  5. He was tired because he ___ (run).

B. Çevir

  1. Ben aradığımda o çoktan çıkmıştı.
  2. Sunucu çöktüğünde iki gündür çalışıyordu.
  3. O güne kadar hiç sunum yapmamıştım.

C. Hatayı bul

  1. I had ate before the meeting.
  2. When I got home, Tom already cooked dinner.
  3. He had been owning the company for ten years.
Cevapları göster
  1. had ended · 2. had been working · 3. had never used · 4. had already approved · 5. had been running
  2. When I called, he/she had already left.
  3. The server had been running for two days when it crashed.
  4. I had never given a presentation until that day.
  5. I had eaten before the meeting.
  6. …Tom had already cooked dinner.
  7. He had owned the company for ten years.

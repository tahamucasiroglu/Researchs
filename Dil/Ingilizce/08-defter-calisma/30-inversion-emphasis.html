Inversion (devrik yapı) ve emphasis (vurgu)

İleri seviye ve resmî İngilizcede bazı kelimeler cümle başına gelince soru sırası kullanılır: Never have I seen… Vurgu için ise cümle ikiye bölünür: It was Ece who fixed it.

İngilizce Defter Rehberi · C1 · 29 Ekim 2026

§ 1Deftere yaz

Deftere yaz — Devrik ve vurgu kalıpları

Formül: Olumsuz/kısıtlayıcı zarf + Aux + S + V · It is/was + X + that/who + … · What + S + V + is/was + X

KalıpÖrnek
Olumlu (vurgu)It was + X + who + VIt was the intern who found the bug.
Olumsuz (devrik)Never + Aux + S + V3Never have I seen such clean code.
Soru gibi sıraNot only + Aux + S + V, but … alsoNot only did it crash, but it also deleted the logs.

Anlam: "Hiç … görmedim", "Hatayı bulan stajyerdi", "Sadece çökmekle kalmadı, …".

§ 2Ne zaman, nasıl kullanılır

  • Never / Rarely / Seldom + devrik. Rarely do we get such feedback. — Böyle geri bildirim nadiren alırız.
  • Not only … but (also). Not only is it fast, but it's also cheap. — Hem hızlı hem ucuz.
  • Hardly / Scarcely … when; No sooner … than. No sooner had I pushed than the build failed. — Gönderir göndermez derleme patladı.
  • Only after / Only when / Only then. Only when we checked the logs did we understand. — Ancak kayıtlara bakınca anladık.
  • Under no circumstances / At no time / In no way. Under no circumstances should you share the key. — Anahtarı hiçbir koşulda paylaşmamalısın.
  • Little + devrik. Little did they know… — Pek bilmiyorlardı ki…
  • if'siz koşul (resmî). Had I known, I'd have come. (= If I had known) · Should you need help, call us. (= If you need) · Were I in your position, … (= If I were)
  • So / Neither + devrik (Konu 22): So do I.
  • Cleft: It is/was … that/who. It was on Friday that the outage happened. — Kesinti cuma günü oldu (başka gün değil).
  • Pseudo-cleft: What … is. What I need is a break. — İhtiyacım olan şey bir mola.
  • Vurgulu do / did. I do understand. — Gerçekten anlıyorum. She did call you! — Seni aradı ama!
  • Here / There + fiil (isim özne). Here comes the bus. (zamirde sıra normal: Here it comes.)

§ 3İpucu kelimeler

  • Devrik tetikleyiciler: never, rarely, seldom, hardly, scarcely, barely, no sooner, not only, not until, only (after/when/then/if), under no circumstances, at no time, in no way, little, nowhere.
  • Devrik yapı resmî yazı ve konuşmada; günlük konuşmada normal sıra daha doğaldır.
  • Zaman do/does/did ile korunur: Never did I think… (past).

§ 4Türklerin sık yaptığı hatalar

YanlışDoğruNeden

Not only it is fast…Not only is it fast…Devrik sıra.
Only then we understood.Only then did we understand.Aux öne.
No sooner had I arrived when……than…no sooner … than.

§ 5Karıştırılan çiftler

Normal sıraDevrik (vurgulu, resmî)
ÖrnekI have never seen this.Never have I seen this.
ÖrnekIf you need help, …Should you need help, …
ÖrnekWe understood only then.Only then did we understand.
It is X that …What … is X
VurguX'i öne çıkarırX'i sona, hedefe koyar
ÖrnekIt's speed that matters.What matters is speed.

§ 6Alıştırma

A. Devrik yapıyla yeniden yaz

  1. I have never worked with such a great team. → Never ___
  2. The app is slow and it is also expensive. → Not only ___
  3. If I had known about the bug, I would have fixed it. → Had ___
  4. We realised the problem only after the release. → Only after the release ___
  5. You must not share this password under any circumstances. → Under no circumstances ___

B. Vurgu (cleft)

  1. Ece fixed the bug. (Ece'yi vurgula) → It ___
  2. I need more time. (What ile) → ___

C. Çevir

  1. Gönderir göndermez hata aldım. (No sooner)
  2. Ancak kayıtları okuyunca sorunu anladık. (Only when)

D. Hatayı bul

  1. Rarely we see such results.
  2. Not until the next day he called.
  3. Should you needed help, contact us.
Cevapları göster
  1. Never have I worked with such a great team. · 2. Not only is the app slow, but it is also expensive. · 3. Had I known about the bug, I would have fixed it. · 4. Only after the release did we realise the problem. · 5. Under no circumstances must you share this password.
  2. It was Ece who fixed the bug. · 7. What I need is more time.
  3. No sooner had I pushed (it) than I got an error.
  4. Only when we read the logs did we understand the problem.
  5. Rarely do we see such results.
  6. Not until the next day did he call.
  7. Should you need help, contact us.
